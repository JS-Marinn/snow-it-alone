test: a probe that can say WHICH ball it measured, and what it found

PRIORITY 5's thread. Every diagnosis of "the anti-soft-lock throw never reaches the machine" so far
was made from POSITIONS read out of `get_nodes_in_group("snowballs")`, which returns the FIRST body
in the group -- and `--tool-ownership` packs a ball earlier in its own run. A trace that cannot say
which object it measured is not a measurement, and at least one earlier conclusion in this session
was drawn from exactly that.

## The probe

`scripts/throw_probe.gd`, run with `--throw-probe`. It takes the ball the player is ACTUALLY
holding before the throw, keeps that exact reference, and prints VELOCITY every frame. Position
says where a ball ended up; velocity says whether it was ever thrown.

    [THROWPROBE] holding a 1.213 kg ball at (-0.24, 1.06, -5.16)
    [THROWPROBE] machine at (0.0, 0.0, -7.6), 2.45 m away horizontally
    frame  0: vel 4.50 m/s (-3.18, -1.29, -2.91)  2.51 m from the mouth  ledger 0.0000 kg/0
    frame 11: vel 5.20 m/s (-3.11, -3.04, -2.84)  2.23 m from the mouth  ledger 0.0000 kg/0

## What it found, which is not what was assumed

  1. THE BALL IS THROWN. Its velocity is 4.50 m/s at the first frame and RISES to 5.20 as it falls.
     It is not inert, not stuck, and not being held. The earlier reading of "0.47 m/s" came from
     following the wrong body.

  2. IT FLIES IN THE WRONG DIRECTION. The velocity is (-3.18, -1.29, -2.91): strong -X, moderate
     -Z, sinking. The machine is at (0, 0, -7.6), so the horizontal direction should be almost
     purely -Z. The throw goes diagonally away from the target and the horizontal gap only closes
     because the ball is falling into the ground, 2.51 m -> 2.23 m over 11 frames.

  3. THE ANNOUNCED SPEED AND THE ACTUAL SPEED DISAGREE. `[THROWDBG]` reports "1.21 kg at 9.00 m/s"
     and `end_carry` sets `linear_velocity = impulse_velocity` with no scaling, yet the ball is
     measured at 4.50 m/s. Half. That is not a rounding difference and it is the number to chase
     next: either the impulse is being reduced between the report and the assignment, or the ball
     is still under the snow-support spring during the first frames of flight and the velocity read
     is the post-step one.

## State

`--tool-ownership` is unchanged at 35 OK / 2 FAIL. The useful change is that the failure is now
described by a measurement that names its subject, with two concrete numbers (a direction and a
speed) that the next step can be built on, instead of by a position trace that may have been
following the wrong ball.

Not wired into `tools/run_batteries.ps1`: this is an instrument, not a battery, and it asserts
nothing. It is registered as a demo flag in `main.gd` and `main_menu.gd` so it can be run.
