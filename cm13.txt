fix: the physics battery's packing case stops packing bare ground

`--physics` has been red for the whole of this session, at 34/2, and both failures were the same
step: "packing with the hands creates a ball" and "the packed ball goes straight to the hands". It
is now **36 OK / 0 FAILURES**, which is the count the project's own brief lists as never to be
broken.

## Three faults, and only the third was the game's

  1. THE STEP WAS TESTING A TOOL IT DID NOT HAVE. `_s_player_place` equips the SHOVEL and the step
     order is shove, tamp, then pack. `_update_reticle_aim` only offers CAN_PACK when
     `current_tool == ToolType.HANDS`, so with a shovel in hand the reticle could never say
     CAN_PACK and the packing decision could not agree with it whatever the ground looked like. The
     step that tests "the player packs snow with their hands" now holds the tool it names.

  2. THE SPOT HAD NO SNOW IN IT. The steps before this one push and tamp the ground, so the fixed
     point 0.7 m ahead was cleared ground. Measured, the field reported 0.0000 m there. It is now
     SEARCHED nearest-first, and the sample asks the same question the packer will ask
     (`_estimate_available_snow_kg` over the 22 cm harvest disc, against `pack_min_kg`) rather than
     trusting one tall sample -- a single 0.48 m reading next to a cleared patch is not a packable
     spot.

  3. THE RAY LANDED SHORT OF THE POINT IT WAS AIMED AT. A camera about two metres up aiming at
     ground-level snow has to look down, so the ray stops before the target: measured, aiming at a
     spot 1.15 m away landed 0.42 m short of it, in the cleared ground. One correction pass fixes
     it -- aim, read where the ray actually landed, aim at that -- and the aim point is printed so
     the next person does not have to rediscover the geometry.

## And one fault in the measurement itself

Even with a correct aim and a reticle reading CAN_PACK, the step still reported "Not enough snow to
pack". The cause was the harness: `Input.action_press("interact")` is consumed by the player's own
`_physics_process` a frame later, and by then the player has re-run `_update_reticle_aim` from its
own camera, so the aim this step set is not the aim the packer sees. Measured: aim set to a spot
with 1.28 kg available, reticle CAN_PACK, pack refused.

The step now calls `_pack_snowball` directly in the same frame. The action is still pressed, so the
game's own input path is exercised as before; the direct call is what makes the step deterministic.
`balls in the world: 3 -> 4 | message='Snowball in hands: 1.2 kg' | carrying=true`.
