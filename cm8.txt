fix: the throw that never aimed, and the test that never looked where it aimed

PRIORITY 5's follow-up. Two real faults found while chasing "the anti-soft-lock delivery lands
nothing", and neither of them was the payout.

## The test was checking a different machine

`tool_ownership_demo.gd` carried `const MACHINE_Z: float = 17.0` -- the TEST scene's machine
position -- and used it when reporting where the throw was aimed. This battery runs in the LEVEL,
where the machine is at `z = -7.6`. So every diagnosis of this failure, including the one written
into the code as a "known blocker" blaming the Playground scene loading, was measuring the flight
against a mouth that was 22 metres away from the one being thrown at.

The trace now reads the machine that exists, via `root.get_node_or_null("DisposalMachine")`, and
reports the distance to IT plus its own ledger (`accepted_kg`, `deliveries`). A battery that
reports on a different object than the one under test is worse than no report.

## The throw was aimed with a guessed constant

The aim was `mouth + 0.35`, and the +0.35 was a guess. The throw is aimed FROM THE CAMERA THROUGH
THE BALL, which sits lower in the hand, so the line already points downwards before gravity is
added: aiming "a little above the mouth" sends the ball at the floor. The machine's own battery
measured the correction at ITS distance -- roughly 0.14 m of lift for a 1.45 m throw -- and the drop
grows with the square of the flight time, so this one, at 2.2 m, now carries `AIM_LIFT = 0.30`
declared as a named constant rather than a number inside a `look_at` call.

## What the trace then showed, which is the part worth keeping

`[THROWDBG] 1.21 kg at 9.00 m/s, 4.4 deg off the crosshair` -- so the throw DOES happen, at the
right speed and aimed within 4.4 degrees. And the ball then moves at **0.47 m/s**: 0.05 m per frame,
x drifting steadily negative, y falling. It is not flying. It is being carried or pushed by
something in the first centimetres after release, and it never covers the 2.76 m to the machine.

That is a defect in the throw or in what the thrower collides with at its own hand, not in the
machine and not in the aim. It is recorded here rather than guessed at, because the next step is to
print the ball's velocity each frame rather than its position, and that is a different measurement
from the one this commit set out to take.
