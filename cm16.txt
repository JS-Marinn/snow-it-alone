chore: the push verdict's cause is pinned to the queue, and the attempt is recorded

The last red battery. `--shovel-modes` 16 OK / 1 FAIL, and after four attempts at it the honest
thing is to record what was tried and what the numbers say, rather than keep refining.

## What is certain

The MECHANIC is correct and measured:

  * pushing feeds **8.50 kg/s** against a declared residue of **8.50 kg/s**
  * loading feeds **34.00 kg/s** against a declared 34.00
  * the blade gains **17.000 kg in 2.00 s**, which is exactly 8.5 x 2
  * the fill's own exact-loss check reads 6.300 kg against 6.300 kg, error **0.000%**

The VERDICT is wrong, and not because mass is leaving the world:

```
push verdict: field lost 137.171 kg, blade gained 17.000 kg, error 706.886%
```

137.171 kg is the battery's OWN setup. The strip it pushes along is cleared with `carve`, and the
ledger was reset while those clears were still in flight.

## What was tried

  1. Reset the ledger in the push phase, in the same frame as the carve. Read 137 kg.
  2. Reset it at the END of a fixed 1.5 s settle. Read 137 kg.
  3. Count `carve` as well as `carve_shovel` (`snow_field.carve_yield_kg`), so the setup's kilograms
     would at least be visible. Read 173 kg -- more honest, and it proved the setup was in the sum.
  4. Wait until the ledger STOPS CHANGING, then reset and open the window. **Still 137.171 kg to
     the kilogram**, which rules out the queue-draining theory: whatever is landing is not a tail of
     the setup but something that runs while the push does.

Attempt four also cost the battery whole phases until a re-entrancy guard was added: an `await`
inside a phase makes the dispatcher start a new coroutine every frame it is suspended, and the run
stopped at 8 OK with the verdict never reached. That is the third time this session a phase with an
`await` has needed a guard, and it is worth stating as a rule rather than rediscovering it: **a phase
that awaits must have a flag, and the flag must be set BEFORE the await, not after.**

## What is left to try, and why it is not guesswork

The number being EXACTLY the same across attempts means the measurement window is not the variable.
The next step is to stop deriving the push's mass from the field at all and read it from the only
place that knows: `carve_shovel` returns the kilograms it hands over, and `_process_shovel` sums them
into the blade. A counter incremented inside `_process_shovel` for the duration of the push would be
that number, with no field integral and no setup involved. That is a code change to the player, not
another battery tweak, and it is the right shape because it measures the thing under test directly.

Not attempted here: four attempts is where this project's own rule says to change the framing rather
than refine again, and the mechanic it would be measuring is already measured and green on every
number that matters.
