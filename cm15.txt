fix: the gate stops reporting a race in its own harness as a crash

THE INTERMITTENT FAILURE THAT HAS BEEN COSTING THIS SESSION ROUNDS IS IN THE MEASURING TOOL, NOT IN
THE GAME.

## What was happening

`tools/run_batteries.ps1` ran a battery, waited for the process to exit, slept 250 ms ONCE, read the
log files ONCE, and looked for the verdict. A process having exited does not mean the OS has flushed
its redirected stdout to disk, so a battery that finished perfectly could be read as

    no verdict printed (crashed?)

Measured: `--reticle-aim` was reported that way by the gate while its own log ended with
`[RETICLE] RESULT: 23 OK / 0 FAIL` and its error log was EMPTY. The same battery passes every time
when run by hand. That signature -- consistent alone, intermittent under the gate -- is a race in the
harness.

It was misread as a crash at least three times in this session and sent investigations in the wrong
direction twice: once toward a `look_at` colinearity warning that turned out to be unrelated, and
once toward GPU device loss.

## The fix

Poll for the verdict for up to two seconds instead of reading once:

    while (no verdict in the logs and within the deadline) { sleep 100ms; re-read }

This does NOT weaken the rule the project follows -- a battery without a verdict is a FAILURE. It
waits for the verdict to arrive; a battery that never writes one is still a failure after two
seconds. After the fix, `--reticle-aim` passes inside the gate at **33 OK / 0 FAIL**.

## And the same shape of flakiness, in a battery

`--physics` then failed one check inside the gate while passing alone: "the packed ball goes straight
to the hands", with the log showing `balls in the world: 3 -> 4` and the message already reading
`Snowball in hands: 1.2 kg` while `carrying` was still false. The ball existed and the check called it
a failure.

Packing is a REQUEST: the field removes the snow, returns the mass, and only then does the ball reach
the hands. The step checked on its scheduled tick, which was enough when run by hand and not always
enough inside the full gate. It now polls for up to 1.5 s until the player is actually carrying.

**Three consecutive runs: 36 OK / 0 FAILURES each time**, and the printed wait shows the real
variation the fix absorbs -- 0.40 s, 0.00 s, 0.40 s. That spread is exactly what was breaking it.
