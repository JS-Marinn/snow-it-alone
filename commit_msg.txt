fix: gathering needs snow close to the aim point, and the reticle proves itself in pixels

The owner's specification, which is what four previous attempts were missing:

    "Gathering snow happens where the reticle is: if I look straight ahead instead of
     down, it must not gather snow."

## The gathering fix

`_find_pack_target` had a gate on "the ray hit something" and on "there is snow at the aim
point". Both passed while looking forward, because looking forward puts the aim point on the
ground METRES away, where there is plenty of snow. The missing requirement was that the aim
point be CLOSE, and PACK_REACH_STRICT (1.3 m) already existed for it and was not applied.

`_aim_within_pack_reach()` is now the single predicate, read by BOTH the reticle state and the
pack, so a reticle that says CAN_PACK cannot disagree with what a tap does.

The door closes BEFORE anything is queued, and that order is the whole point: an earlier attempt
gated the queued harvest instead and produced a hole in the snow with no ball, which is mass
leaving the world unaccounted. Measured now, in --reticle-aim: looking down leaves a local hole
of +0.0245 m and hands over a 1.213 kg ball; looking forward, looking at the sky and looking at
cleared ground all leave the local height unchanged to within 0.0000 m and create no ball.

## The reticle, measured in pixels

The dot was white on snow: centre luma 0.96 against snow at 0.99, three per cent of contrast. It
was drawn, centred to the pixel, and invisible by construction, which is exactly what the owner
reported while the geometry log said it was fine. It is now a dark dot on a light ring, always
visible, coloured by state, and it is verified by opening the PNG the game writes:

  reticle_down:    dot at (640, 360) rgb (0.855, 0.192, 0.145) luma 0.397 | snow 0.986 | contrast 0.588 | redness 0.710
  reticle_forward: dot at (640, 360) rgb (0.584, 0.596, 0.631) luma 0.604 | snow 1.000 | contrast 0.396 | redness -0.025

## The build stamp

Every run now starts with "[BUILD] <hash>", from build_hash.txt, written by
tools/stamp_build.ps1. "Am I running the code I think I am?" has cost this project time twice
and there was no way to answer it from a screenshot.

## Batteries updated, not deleted

--hand-pack, --reticle-act, --disposal-machine, --tool-ownership and --phys-demo all packed by
hand WITHOUT AIMING, which is the behaviour the owner reported as a bug. They now aim first, and
the two that asserted extended reach at 1.7 m and 2.0 m assert the opposite: that nothing is
gathered past the strict reach. Their comments say the assertion changed and why.

Measured: --reticle-aim 23 OK / 0 FAIL (new). --hand-pack 12 OK / 0. --reticle-act 33 OK / 0
when run alone. --tool-ownership 38 OK / 2 FAIL, and it used to HANG rather than fail.

## Not green, and why

Three batteries still fail, all on the same thread and none of them the reticle:

  * --tool-ownership and --disposal-machine: the anti-soft-lock check packs and throws, and the
    ball never reaches the machine. The cause is measured: the PLAYGROUND SCENE IS LOADED ON TOP
    of the level during the run ("[PG] disposal machine placed at ..." in the same log), which
    takes the tree away, so the ball stops existing and the machine registers no delivery.
  * --phys-demo: 34/2, its rig places the player at (0.8, 0, 1.17) where the new aim is out of
    reach and the reticle reads OFF.

This commit is the gather-and-reticle fix, which is measured and green in its own battery. The
scene-switch fault above is documented in the code where it bites and is the next thing to fix.
