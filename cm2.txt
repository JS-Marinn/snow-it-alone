fix: one world at a time, and the aim ceiling stops rolling the camera

Two defects found by a structural audit of the project, both of which were costing real time
without ever being named.

## A battery was building a second game inside the running one

`tool_ownership_demo.gd` state 6 instantiated `res://scenes/playground.tscn` and added it as a
CHILD OF THE LEVEL, from inside a physics tick. That is two complete worlds alive at once -- each
with its own player, its own snow field and its own GPU simulation -- and the new scene's `_ready`
then placed its own disposal machine, wired its own player and granted its own tools.

It was not a local problem for that one battery. Other batteries' logs contained
"[PG] disposal machine placed at ..." in the middle of a LEVEL run, which is this line, and it is
the cause this project wrote down as "the anti-soft-lock chain": the tree the thrown ball lived in
was replaced underneath it, so the delivery could not land and the coin never moved.

The property under test is "the Playground grants every tool at start". That is a contract and it
is now checked as one: the scene's own wiring must call `grant_all_tools`, and granting must
actually give a fresh player all four tools and leave it without them beforehand. No second world,
no scene load in a physics step.

--tool-ownership: 38 checks -> 35, still 2 failing, and the two that fail are the anti-soft-lock
delivery rather than the ownership contract this battery exists for.

## The sky target was directly overhead, which rolled the camera

`reticle_aim_demo.gd` aimed at `(0, 40, 6)` from a player at `(0, 0.32, 6)`: exactly vertical.
`look_at` warns "Target and up vectors are colinear" and picks an arbitrary roll, and that warning
was the ONLY line in the battery's error log on the run the gate reported as "no verdict printed
(crashed?)" -- an intermittent failure that looked like a crash in the gate and passed when run
alone. The target is now slightly off-axis.

--reticle-aim passes inside the gate again (23 OK / 0 FAIL), which it did not before: the gate
went from 5 red to 4.

## Free win: Godot was importing duplicates of its own code

`backup/` and `scratch_wip/` had no `.gdignore`, so the engine imported 4,806 lines of duplicated
GDScript including a second 1,755-line `player_controller.gd` declaring the same class. Nothing
live references either tree (the only match is `tools/restore_flat_snow.ps1`, which reads by disk
path and not through `res://`), so both now carry an empty `.gdignore`. One empty file each.

## Still failing, and now with a better name for it

  * --tool-ownership and --disposal-machine: the anti-soft-lock delivery does not land. The second
    world is gone, so the remaining cause is the delivery itself and not the tree.
  * --phys-demo: 34/2, its rig stands where the new aim is out of reach.
  * --shovel-modes: 16/1, the push's exact-loss verdict, which is a flaw in the battery's
    measurement window and not in the mechanic.
  * --disposal-machine ALSO loses the Vulkan device in its last phase ("Vulkan device was lost"),
    after a large teleport of the player. Intermittent, driver-level, and recorded here rather
    than chased: it is the only device loss in the project's logs.
